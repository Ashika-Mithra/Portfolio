About Me

I am an cybersecurity enthusiast who enjoys understanding how systems work under the hood and how they can be protected in real world environments. With a background in Electronics and Communication Engineering, along with a Postgraduate Diploma in Wireless Networking, I’ve built a strong technical foundation that naturally led me into the field of cybersecurity.

My interest grew from curiosity about how data moves across networks and how attackers exploit weaknesses in systems. To turn that curiosity into practical skill, I focus on hands-on learning through cybersecurity home labs and real world simulations.

I have built and explored projects such as Active Directory environments to understand enterprise identity systems, SIEM setups using Wazuh for security monitoring, and vulnerable machine labs like Metasploitable for learning exploitation and defense techniques. I also experiment with building honeypots, analyzing network traffic, and studying threat intelligence sources to understand attacker behavior and defensive strategies.

Right now, I am continuously learning and improving my skills in SOC operations, threat detection, and network security. My goal is to grow into a cybersecurity professional who can contribute effectively to security operations and help organizations defend against evolving cyber threats.
